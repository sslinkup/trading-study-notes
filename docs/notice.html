Attribution and scope

Source of the ideas

The material summarised here comes from the public teaching videos published by BareNakedCrypto on YouTube:

https://www.youtube.com/@BareNakedCrypto

Those videos, their transcripts, and their visual content are not included on this site and remain the property of their creator. Every page links back to the relevant moment in the original video so that the source can be verified directly.

This site is non-commercial. It has no advertisements, affiliate links, paywalls, donations, or tracking scripts of its own. The only outbound links are to the original source videos and to the license and attribution pages.

The small play button beside a timestamp loads a video embedded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com) only when you click it — nothing is embedded on page load. That player belongs to YouTube and the video's creator: it may display YouTube's own ads or set cookies, and it is covered by YouTube's terms rather than this site's policy. If a creator has disabled embedding, the player will show an error; the timestamp link beside it will still open the video on YouTube.

What is on this site

  • Original prose written as a study aid: summaries, organisation, and cross-references of the ideas and factual settings presented in the videos.
  • Links and timestamps pointing to the original videos.
  • No transcript text and no verbatim quotations from the videos.

Facts, numbers, indicator settings, and ideas are not protected by copyright. The expression on these pages — the wording, structure, and organisation — is original and is released under CC0 1.0.

What is deliberately excluded

  • Full video transcripts, which are copies of the creators' speech.
  • Long direct quotations from the videos.
  • The original videos themselves, thumbnails, and channel artwork.

Corrections

If you believe any material on this site is incorrectly attributed or should not be here, open an issue or contact the site maintainer. Material that does not belong here will be removed.
