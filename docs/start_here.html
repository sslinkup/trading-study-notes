Trading Study Notes

A free, self-contained study guide built from public trading lessons. It is written in original prose and released into the public domain — see License and Attribution & scope.

How to use this site

  • Every highlighted timestamp opens the original video at that exact moment, so any claim here can be checked against its source.
  • Plain video URLs are clickable and open in a new tab.
  • The pages are static HTML. No account, tracking, or internet connection is needed to read the text.

Contents

  1. Foundations — mindset, bankroll and journaling — the core premise, starting small, self-data, sizing, withdrawals, rules and the learning routine.
  2. Mistakes and risk — failure patterns, profit retention, stop-loss philosophy, liquidity, overtrading and toolkit discipline.
  3. Lessons and philosophy — the game metaphor, sources of edge, happy decisions, leverage, shorting and the chart toolkit.
  4. Naked RSI, stochastic RSI and NKED — exact levels and settings, divergence, multi-timeframe method and a step-by-step workflow.
  5. Indicators and chart reading — levels and volume clusters, EMAs, Ichimoku, Keltner/Watson and how the tools combine.
  6. Volume, order flow and timing — volume thresholds, delta, order-book traps, timeframe theory and entry timing.

A note on ownership

The linked videos belong to their creator. These pages are an independent summary with pointers back to the source, not a copy or a replacement for the videos. If you want the full detail, follow the timestamps.
