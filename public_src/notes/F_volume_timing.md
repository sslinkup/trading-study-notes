# Volume, Order Flow and Timing

*Original study notes. The linked videos are the original sources and remain their authors' property. Click a timestamp to verify a claim at its source.*

## 1. Volume as the primary evidence

The material treats volume as more reliable than chart narratives: price levels matter, but the size of the participation at those levels determines whether a move is real. Technical analysis is framed as betting analysis, not prediction.

Order-flow data has to be observed continuously. A short visit once or twice a day misses the accumulation that matters. The setup described uses a dedicated machine or virtual private server running the tool for long stretches, and the suggested quote currency is USD rather than USDT.

Sources: *How to Use Aggr.Trade — Volume to Predict the Market* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0); *How to Approach Timeframe* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoIr0w6l8gc).

## 2. Thresholds

The 2024 setup uses a rolling fifteen-minute window — a continuous fifteen minutes of trading, not a fifteen-minute candle. The 2025 update raises the numbers:

- roughly **50 million in five minutes** is the minimum for meaningful activity;
- roughly **300 million in fifteen minutes** indicates good activity;
- one side exceeding the other by more than **300 million** indicates clear directional pressure;
- breaking a major resistance can require close to **a billion** in aggressive shorts, for example near ETH 4,250 or BTC 107.2.

These are reference figures from the source videos, not universal constants; they are repeated here so the timestamp links can be used to check the context.

Sources: *How to Use Aggr.Trade* ([02:17], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0); *How to Use Aggr.Trade — 2025 Update* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KPD2ElzbQnc).

## 3. Delta and fake moves

The tape shows the battle between aggressive buyers and sellers. When shorts keep piling up while longs also build, the move is contested; a rapid change in the ratio — such as a reading dropping from the low hundreds to around seventy — is used as a signal to take profit.

Absorption matters: if large volume hits a level without price moving, the level is being defended. Liquidation hunts push price through obvious clusters of stops before the move resumes.

Sources: *How to Use Aggr.Trade* ([11:56], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0); ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0).

## 4. Order books are a trap

The source material warns against using the order book as a primary signal: visible liquidity is often moved or spoofed, and traders who rely on it tend to be stopped out. The advice is to use executed volume and delta instead.

Sources: *How to Use Aggr.Trade* ([05:42], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0).

## 5. Altcoins lead; ETH joins Bitcoin

Smaller coins are described as leading indicators: they tend to dump before Bitcoin and to sell off first when risk appetite changes. The 2025 update adds that volume leadership shifted toward Ethereum, so both BTC and ETH are checked before a timing decision.

Sources: *How to Use Aggr.Trade — 2025 Update* ([02:08], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KPD2ElzbQnc); *How to Use Aggr.Trade* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0).

## 6. Worked examples

A January 2024 BTC short around 40.4k/40.2k is used to show taking profit when the aggressive-volume ratio collapsed. A 2025 example marks the bottom of the Keltner/Watson area near 4,306 on the twenty-seven-minute chart and notes that a reversal would need a volume increase on both Bitcoin and Ethereum.

Sources: *How to Use Aggr.Trade* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EO6nFd5-Fl0); *How to Use Aggr.Trade — 2025 Update* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KPD2ElzbQnc).

## 7. Timeframe theory

The ladder is built on 3, 6 and 9, with the "times nine" rule connecting a candle to the timeframe above or below it (three three-minute candles make a nine-minute candle; three nine-minute candles make a twenty-seven-minute candle). The proposed translation factor between a timeframe and the next is roughly nine to twelve candles.

Practical guidance is to start on higher timeframes and work down. A lower-timeframe signal is only an entry mechanism for a story already established above it.

Sources: *How to Approach Timeframe* ([07:32], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoIr0w6l8gc); *Stochastic RSI — Deeper Breakdown* ([07:45], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iOXOgQCX5f4).

## 8. Writing the story

Before entering, write down: the goal for the trade, the side, the timeframe, the higher-timeframe context, the invalidation level, and the volume condition that would confirm or deny the idea. The oscillator's role is to describe energy — a fast stochastic can stay extended for around eight or nine candles, so extension alone is not a reversal signal.

Sources: *How to Approach Timeframe* ([08:07], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoIr0w6l8gc); *Part 1 — Timing Your Positions* ([04:48], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyGIkmMb-Sw).

## 9. Timing entries and exits

A good entry is close to a structural level with room to place the stop beyond the noise. The material gives an example of a long near 3,292 with an invalidation near 3,381 and notes that an instrument like ETH can often be re-entered within a narrow price range, so waiting for the right tick is practical.

Profit-taking is planned at the same time: close part of the position into strength and give the remainder a wider stop. Focus on one coin — the first step toward better timing is knowing one instrument well rather than watching ten.

Sources: *Part 1 — Timing Your Positions* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NyGIkmMb-Sw); *Lesson 2 — Alpha That Will Save You Thousands* ([28:41], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rLdY77E8KzA); *Key Thoughts Going Into 2026* ([00:00], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ti3JhXEY9UM).

## 10. Practice loop

Timing improves through experience, a journal, and controlled size. Record the reason for each entry and exit, review the outcome against the original story, and change one thing at a time.

Sources: *Lesson 1 — Introduction to Trading* ([13:34], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKJPwG14y2M); *The Key to Success* ([18:08], https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UfxEboq-tOM).
