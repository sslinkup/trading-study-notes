## What is on this site

- Original prose written as a study aid: summaries, organisation, and cross-references of the ideas and factual settings presented in the videos.
- Links and timestamps pointing to the original videos.
- No transcript text and no verbatim quotations from the videos.

Facts, numbers, indicator settings, and ideas are not protected by copyright. The expression on these pages — the wording, structure, and organisation — is original and is released under [CC0 1.0](license.md).

## What is deliberately excluded

- Full video transcripts, which are copies of the creators' speech.
- Long direct quotations from the videos.
- The original videos themselves, thumbnails, and channel artwork.
