# Trading Study Notes

A free, self-contained study guide built from public trading lessons. It is written in original prose and released into the public domain — see [License](license.md) and [Attribution & scope](notice.md).

## How to use this site

- Every **highlighted timestamp** opens the original video at that exact moment, so any claim here can be checked against its source.
- Plain video URLs are clickable and open in a new tab.
- The pages are static HTML. No account, tracking, or internet connection is needed to read the text.

## Contents

1. [Foundations — mindset, bankroll and journaling](notes/A_foundations.md) — the core premise, starting small, self-data, sizing, withdrawals, rules and the learning routine.
2. [Mistakes and risk](notes/B_mistakes.md) — failure patterns, profit retention, stop-loss philosophy, liquidity, overtrading and toolkit discipline.
3. [Lessons and philosophy](notes/C_lessons.md) — the game metaphor, sources of edge, happy decisions, leverage, shorting and the chart toolkit.
4. [Naked RSI, stochastic RSI and NKED](notes/D_stochastic.md) — exact levels and settings, divergence, multi-timeframe method and a step-by-step workflow.
5. [Indicators and chart reading](notes/E_indicators.md) — levels and volume clusters, EMAs, Ichimoku, Keltner/Watson and how the tools combine.
6. [Volume, order flow and timing](notes/F_volume_timing.md) — volume thresholds, delta, order-book traps, timeframe theory and entry timing.
